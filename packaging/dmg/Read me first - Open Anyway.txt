C3D MCP - first launch
======================

1. Drag "C3D MCP" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications. macOS will say it cannot verify the app. That is expected:
   the app is signed with its own certificate, not with an Apple Developer ID.
3. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash"), then open
      System Settings > Privacy & Security
   scroll down to the message about "C3D MCP" and click "Open Anyway". Enter your password.
4. Open C3D MCP again and click "Open". You only do this once per Mac.

Prefer not to click through? Open Terminal and paste the one-line installer from the project's
release page. It downloads the same app straight to /Applications, and macOS does not ask.

Everything else (installing the bridge in Cheetah3D, connecting Claude and other AI assistants,
permissions, removing older versions) is done inside the app. Nothing needs Homebrew, Node or Terminal.
